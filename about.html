About

CS student at KFU. I read binaries and write about what I find.

Background

I'm a Computer Science and Data Analysis student at King Faisal University, graduating February 2027. I spend most of my time reading disassembly, mostly Windows binaries, mostly the parts that touch the kernel. The write-ups here are the record of that work: what I looked at, what I found, and what I got wrong.

Focus

Right now I'm working on Windows internals and anti-cheat research on the side while working full-time as an IT support specialist at the Ministry of Justice.
